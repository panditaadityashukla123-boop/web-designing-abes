Wave Optics - Complete Notes + PYQs + HOTS

Chapter Overview

Wave Optics explains the wave nature of light through:

  • Huygens Principle
  • Interference
  • Young Double Slit Experiment (YDSE)
  • Diffraction
  • Polarisation

1. Huygens Principle

Every point on a wavefront acts as a source of secondary wavelets. The new wavefront is the forward envelope of these wavelets.

2. Wavefront

  • Spherical Wavefront - Produced by a point source.
  • Plane Wavefront - Produced by a distant source.
  • Cylindrical Wavefront - Produced by a line source.

3. Interference of Light

Redistribution of light intensity due to superposition of coherent light waves.

Conditions for Sustained Interference

  • Same frequency
  • Constant phase difference
  • Coherent sources
  • Nearly equal amplitudes

4. Young Double Slit Experiment (YDSE)

Fringe Width

β = λD / d

Where:

  • β = Fringe Width
  • λ = Wavelength
  • D = Distance between slit and screen
  • d = Distance between slits

Bright Fringe

Path Difference = nλ

Dark Fringe

Path Difference = (2n+1)λ/2

5. Diffraction

Bending of light around obstacles and apertures.

Width of Central Maximum = 2λD / a
  • Central maximum is widest.
  • Central maximum is brightest.
  • Intensity decreases away from center.

6. Polarisation

Restriction of vibrations of light to a single plane.

Since light can be polarised, it proves that light is a transverse wave.

Applications

  • Sunglasses
  • Camera Filters
  • LCD Screens
  • 3D Movies

Interference vs Diffraction

Interference Diffraction
Requires two coherent sources Can occur with a single slit
Equal fringe width Unequal fringe width
Fringes have nearly equal intensity Central maximum strongest
Superposition effect Bending effect
